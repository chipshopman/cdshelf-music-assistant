CD Shelf 0.7.6

- Added an A-Z jump strip above the shelf for fast navigation through large libraries.
- Letter jumps follow the active sort mode: artist initials when sorted by artist, album initials when sorted by album.
- Added a # jump for titles beginning with numbers or symbols.
- Letters with no matching albums are automatically disabled.
- Retains the 0.7.5 bottom-bar Previous / Play-Pause / Next controls.
- Volume control and automatic screensaver mode remain removed.
